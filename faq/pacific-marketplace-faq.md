# Pacific Marketplace Help Center FAQ

Pacific Marketplace is a fictional online store selling home goods, electronics and gifts to customers in the United States. This FAQ is the source of truth for support replies. Last reviewed: October 2026.

## faq-01: How do I check when my order will arrive?

Sign in and go to **Account > Orders**, then select the order to see its status and estimated delivery date. Once an order ships, you also receive an email with a tracking link, usually within 1 business day of purchase. Standard orders arrive in 3–7 business days after shipping. If your tracking hasn't updated for 5 business days, contact support with your order number and we will open a carrier investigation.

## faq-02: Can I change or correct an order after placing it?

You can edit items, quantities or the shipping address within **2 hours** of placing an order, from **Account > Orders > Edit order**. After 2 hours, or once the order status shows "Shipped", the order can no longer be changed. In that case you can return unwanted items under our return policy (faq-05), or contact support and we will try to redirect the package with the carrier, which is not guaranteed.

## faq-03: How do I add or change my shipping address?

Go to **Account > Addresses** to add, edit or delete addresses. You can save up to 5 addresses and mark one as the default. Changing a saved address does not update orders that are already placed; to change the address on an existing order, see faq-02. We ship only to addresses in the 50 US states, including PO boxes for Standard shipping. We do not ship internationally.

## faq-04: What shipping methods do you offer?

| Method | Delivery time after shipping | Cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Standard | 3–7 business days | $5.99, free on orders of $50 or more |
| Expedited | 2–3 business days | $12.99 |
| Next Day | 1 business day (order by 12 p.m. Pacific) | $24.99 |

Gold and Platinum members get free Expedited shipping on every order (faq-10). Next Day is not available to PO boxes, Alaska or Hawaii.

## faq-05: What is your return and refund policy?

You can return most items within **30 days of delivery** if they are unused and in original packaging. Start a return at **Account > Orders > Return items** to get a prepaid label. Electronics that have been opened, gift cards and personalized items cannot be returned unless they arrived damaged or defective. Return shipping is free for damaged, defective or incorrect items; otherwise a $6.99 return fee is deducted from your refund.

## faq-06: How long does a refund take?

We inspect returns within 3 business days of receiving them, then issue the refund to your original payment method. Refunds to cards appear in **5–10 business days** depending on your bank. Refunds to Pacific Marketplace store credit appear within 24 hours. You can check refund status at **Account > Orders > Refund status**. If more than 10 business days have passed since you received a "Refund issued" email, contact support with your order number.

## faq-07: Can I get compensation for a late, damaged or wrong delivery?

Yes. If your order arrives damaged or incorrect, report it within **7 days of delivery** with photos at **Account > Orders > Report a problem**, and we will send a replacement or a full refund including shipping. If a Next Day or Expedited order arrives late, we refund the shipping fee. We do not offer cash compensation beyond the value of the order; goodwill store credit is decided case by case by a support agent.

## faq-08: Which payment methods do you accept?

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay and Pacific Marketplace gift cards. We do not accept cash on delivery, checks or cryptocurrency. If a payment is declined, check that your billing address matches your card statement, then try again or use another method. We will **never** ask for your full card number, security code or account PIN by email or chat.

## faq-09: How do I get an invoice for my order?

Invoices are available for every order at **Account > Orders > Download invoice** as a PDF, once the order has shipped. Invoices show the order number, items, taxes and the last 4 digits of the payment card. If you need an invoice with a business name or tax ID, add it at **Account > Billing details** before placing the order; we cannot edit invoices after they are issued.

## faq-10: What account types do you offer, and how do I switch?

| Account | Price | Benefits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Standard | Free | Order history, saved addresses, newsletter |
| Gold | $9.99/month or $99/year | Free Expedited shipping, 45-day returns |
| Platinum | $19.99/month or $199/year | Gold benefits plus free Next Day shipping and priority support |

Switch plans at **Account > Membership**. Upgrades take effect immediately and are prorated. Downgrades take effect at the end of the current billing period. Each account belongs to one person; to open an account for someone else, they must sign up with their own email address.

## faq-11: Are there fees to cancel a membership?

Monthly Gold and Platinum memberships can be cancelled anytime at **Account > Membership > Cancel** with no fee; benefits continue until the end of the paid month. Annual memberships cancelled within 14 days of purchase get a full refund. After 14 days, annual memberships are refunded for the unused months minus an **early termination fee of $20**. Cancelling a membership does not close your account; see faq-12.

## faq-12: How do I close my account?

Go to **Account > Settings > Close account**. Before closing, cancel any active membership (faq-11) and make sure no orders or returns are in progress, because we cannot process returns for closed accounts. Closing is permanent after 30 days; within that window you can reactivate by signing in. Unused store credit is forfeited when the account is permanently closed, so spend or transfer it first.

## faq-13: I can't sign in, sign up, or I forgot my PIN. What do I do?

To reset your password, select **Forgot password** on the sign-in page. Your account PIN, used to confirm phone support calls, can be reset at **Account > Security > Reset PIN** after signing in, or by phone after verifying your identity. If you can't create an account, make sure the email isn't already registered and try a different browser. If sign-in or sign-up still fails, contact support and include a screenshot of any error message. Support agents will never ask you for your password or PIN.

Platinum members are routed to priority support with an average wait under 2 minutes. To update your name, email or phone number yourself, go to **Account > Personal details**.

## faq-15: How do I leave feedback or manage the newsletter?

To review a product, go to **Account > Orders**, select the item and choose **Write a review**; reviews are published after moderation within 2 business days. For feedback about our service, use **Help > Send feedback**. To subscribe to the weekly newsletter, check the box at **Account > Communication preferences**; to unsubscribe, uncheck it there or click **Unsubscribe** at the bottom of any newsletter email. Changes take effect within 48 hours.
